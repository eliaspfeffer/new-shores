## Prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: transparent 4 by 4 sprite atlas for circular command medallions in the browser strategy game New Shores
>
> Primary request: Create exactly sixteen separate hand-painted comic command icons arranged in a perfectly even 4 columns by 4 rows grid. Each cell contains one centered pictogram inside the same round ivory-and-brass medallion rim, with generous safe padding and no overlap. Cell order left-to-right, top-to-bottom: 1 food category — basket with bread, fish and apple; 2 mining and metal — dark coal rock, iron ingot and small anvil; 3 seafaring — cream-red Roman-inspired ship beside anchor; 4 basic professions — two friendly settlers carrying a crate and hammer; 5 all military — sword, bow and small shield; 6 find settlers — magnifying glass over a settler silhouette; 7 recruit specialists — crossed shovel and geology hammer; 8 carriers — sturdy settler holding a wooden crate; 9 builders — builder with hammer and plank; 10 pioneers — pioneer with shovel and red border stake; 11 geologists — geologist examining striped ore rock with hammer; 12 mark/select — dotted selection rectangle with bright pointer; 13 assign destination — bold directional arrow landing on a small map pin; 14 halt — raised stop hand in front of a square stop marker; 15 return to carrier — specialist walking toward a crate carrier with curved return arrow; 16 carrier reserve — three settlers behind a small shield.
>
> Style/medium: cozy detailed hand-painted European comic strategy-game UI, warm parchment ivory, brass gold, dark brown outlines, subtle upper-left highlights, consistent with existing New Shores medallions
>
> Composition/framing: exact regular grid, all circles identical size and centered, icons readable at 48 to 90 pixels
>
> Constraints: genuinely transparent background outside medallions; no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark; exactly 16 cells and exactly one medallion per cell; no objects crossing cell boundaries; high contrast silhouettes; square image

This is original project artwork informed by the already documented Settlers IV menu references, not extracted or pixel-identical original artwork.
